Privacy

This website describes KaiTech's products. It has no accounts, no cookies of ours and no analytics. The only thing it collects is what you type into the demo request form.

The demo request form

When you send the form we store your name, business, email, phone number if you gave one, the product you chose and your message. We use them to reply and arrange your demo, and for nothing else. We do not sell them or pass them to anyone else.

Supabase stores the request for us. We send two emails through our Gmail account: one to us with your details, and one to you confirming it arrived. If the form shows a Cloudflare Turnstile check, Cloudflare uses it to tell people from spam bots.

Showing you the page

  • Hosting. The site is served by Vercel, which keeps standard server logs such as your IP address and the pages requested.
  • Fonts. The typefaces load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when they load.

When you email or call

We keep your message and contact details so we can reply, on the same terms as the form.

Our products

KaiWorkforce, BillZilla, KaiInspect and Drivo hold business data for the companies that use them. That data is covered by each company's agreement with KaiTech, not by this page.

Questions or removal

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email kaitechfj@gmail.com.

Questions we get asked

How much does it cost?

It depends on which products you use and how many staff you have. Book a demo and we'll send you a quote within 24 hours.

Can customers pay by M-PAiSA or MyCash?

Yes. BillZilla records bank transfers, cash, cash on delivery, cheques, M-PAiSA and MyCash, including part payments.

Can I use just one product?

Yes. Each one works on its own. Add another later and it connects to what you already have.

Does it handle VAT, FNPF and PAYE?

Yes. BillZilla handles VAT on quotes, invoices and credit notes. KaiWorkforce works out FNPF and PAYE from your configured rates on every pay run.

Is my data safe?

It's stored with established cloud providers and encrypted in transit. Each business's data is kept separate, and you decide who on your team can see what.

Can you move my data from Xero or spreadsheets?

Yes. Tell us what you use now and we'll plan the move with you.

I'm not in Suva. Can I still use it?

Yes. Everything runs online, anywhere in Fiji. If you're near Suva, we're happy to come and see you.

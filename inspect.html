What it does

Room by room

An auditor walks the site and marks each checklist item Pass or Fail. Every Fail needs a photo and becomes a problem with a due date.

Works with no signal

The inspection saves on the phone after every tap and sends when the signal comes back.

Fixes with proof

The cleaner gets a WhatsApp link, opens it with no account, and photographs the fix. The auditor approves it or sends it back.

Reports for both sides

Customers get a clean report link. The company gets the full detail: every item, photo, time to fix and the most repeated problems.

What it does

Quotes to invoices, in order

A quote becomes an invoice in one step. Numbers are given when you finalise, so there are never gaps, and every invoice uses one of seven A4 layouts with your logo.

Paid the way Fiji pays

Record bank transfers, cash, cash on delivery, cheques, M-PAiSA and MyCash, including part payments.

Stock that keeps up

Stock moves when an invoice is finalised, voided or credited, and load sheets are ready for the delivery run.

Chasing done for you

Customers get emailed invoices, statements and automatic payment reminders. Prices below a product's minimum wait for the owner's approval.
